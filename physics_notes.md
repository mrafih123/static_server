Area under I–t graph = Q (charge)

## Conductors not connected with battery (in the absence of EF)

ēs have random thermal motion

- Thermal vel = 10^5 m/s (large)
- Avg thermal vel = 0
- No net current

## Conductors connected with battery (in the presence of EF)

- EF is set up inside the condr
- ē drift from −ve potential to +ve potential
  in the opp. dirⁿ of E
- Drift vel = 10^-4 m/s (small)
- Drift :— motion of ēs **under the influence of EF**

> *Note Dirⁿ of E → +ve to −ve
